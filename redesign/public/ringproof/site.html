01The gapWhy test calls

Your reports say the call was answered. They don't say the card was blocked.

Queue time and handle time describe the call centre. They say nothing about whether the customer got what they rang for. Ringproof checks the outcome, on a real call, from the customer's side of the line.

02RunsProduct · 1 / 4

One test call.
Everything that was said.

Each run ends with a plain answer: did the task get done? Behind it sit the recording, the transcript and the six checks the caller worked through, so nobody has to argue about what was said.

Outcome

Pass or fail, with the minute it happened

“Card frozen at 03:48” is a fact you can replay. So is “Call dropped at 0:42”.

Checklist

Six checks per run

Reach an agent, explain the problem, pass identity, block the card, order a replacement, get the SMS.

Recording

Interruptions and slow replies marked

Jump to the second the agent talked over the caller, or took 620 ms to answer.

What to fix

A short list, not a report

Three things to change before the next run, each linked to its moment in the call.

03ScenariosProduct · 2 / 4

Write the task the way a customer would say it.

A scenario is a goal, the checks that prove it was reached, and the test data the caller needs. Add a few phrasings and the caller asks in different words each time, like real people do.
