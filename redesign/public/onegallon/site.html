FAQ

Good questions.

How does it know how much is in the glass?

The scan finds the glass, estimates its size from its shape and reads the water line. You always see the amount before it is added.

Does it count coffee, tea or juice?

Yes. Anything you drink is logged; you choose in settings whether it counts in full towards the goal.

Is a gallon right for me?

It is where the app starts, not a rule. Set any goal from 40 to 200 oz, in ounces or millilitres.

What happens to my photos?

They are read on your phone and thrown away. Only the number of ounces is kept.
